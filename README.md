# 📊 Supply Chain & Order Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes order-level and supply chain data to extract meaningful business insights. The dataset includes orders, products, customers, and warehouse-related information.

Since the data does not represent real-time stock inventory, I applied data modeling techniques using Power Pivot to simulate operational behavior and analyze demand patterns, order status, and product performance.

The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable insights that support better business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze product performance (fast-moving vs slow-moving products)
* Monitor order status (pending vs canceled)
* Identify revenue risks based on order behavior
* Evaluate demand trends over time
* Understand top contributing customers
* Detect supply gaps and operational inefficiencies

## 📈 Key Insights

* A small number of products generate the majority of demand
* Order status (pending/canceled) has a direct impact on revenue stability
* Demand fluctuates over time, affecting operational planning
* Top customers account for the highest consumption levels
* Certain products show consistent high performance across periods
